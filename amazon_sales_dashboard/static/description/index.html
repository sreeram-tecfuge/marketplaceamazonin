Amazon Sales Dashboard & Workflow Manager

Dynamic OWL JS Analytics, Automated CSV/Excel Processing & Accounting Integration

The Amazon Sales Dashboard module converts Amazon transaction reports (CSV / Excel) into automated Odoo workflows including Sale Orders, Stock Deliveries, Customer Invoices, and Credit Notes.

  • Dynamic Dashboard: KPI Cards, Revenue Charts, Refund Analysis, Top ASIN Breakdown.
  • Single & Bulk Actions: Process shipments into Sale Orders, Pickings, and Invoices with 1-click.
  • Refund & Replacement Management: Link refunds to original orders or process replacement orders automatically.
  • GST & Tax Automation: Indian Tax (CGST, SGST, IGST, UTGST) calculation and HSN/SAC support.
